Shared Anubis runtime helpers were absent from the extraction. The port
provides LiquidBounce-backed settings, events, friends, command completion,
rotation, combat and dialog adapters. The recovered macro routines and
settings are retained, but the missing helpers' exact original behavior
cannot be established from the supplied files.
