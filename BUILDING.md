Donut Schematic Builder uses your enabled Litematica placements.

1. Install Litematica and MaLiLib for Minecraft 26.3 alongside LiquidBounce and this addon. The builder appears in the Donut category when both mods are installed.
2. Load a schematic in Litematica, position it, and enable the placement and the subregions you want to build.
3. Open Donut Schematic Builder's settings before enabling it. Buying from the auction house and selling leftovers default to enabled. Set your listing and total spending limits, or turn these options off to use your existing inventory.
4. Supply the required materials, food, and any enabled scaffold or bucket items, then enable the module. Chat Feedback reports progress and any unresolved work.

The builder checks placement states and server updates, stages inventory, places temporary supports, configures crafter slots, and handles enabled fluid work. Place TNT defaults to off. Walk With Baritone is optional and requires a compatible Baritone installation.

Work is scanned in small windows, including large schematics. Unavailable schematic data or unresolved placement work is reported rather than counted as finished. Disabling the module stops building and attempts to restore its staged inventory while the same world and connection remain available.
